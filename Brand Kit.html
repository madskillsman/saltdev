09 Voice & Tone
Principles
Professional
Authoritative but approachable. Never cold or robotic.
Plain language
Technical terms used sparingly, only when they add clarity.
Direct
Short sentences, active voice, no hedging.
Outcomes first
Talk about results for the client, not features of the service.
Pronouns & casing
We / our
Refers to Salt Development. Conversational, not corporate.
You / your
Refers to the client. Respectful but direct.
Sentence case
Headings and body copy in sentence case. Not Title Case Everywhere.
Brand name
Salt Development — both words capitalised. Never "SALT".
Do write
→"We build websites that rank and convert."
→"Your site should work as hard as you do."
→"SEO that actually moves the needle — no guesswork, no fluff."
→"Let's talk about what's holding your site back."
→"A short call is all it takes to find out what your site needs."
Avoid
—Jargon for its own sake: "synergistic digital ecosystems"
—Hedging: "we might be able to help possibly improve…"
—Overselling: "guaranteed #1 rankings in 30 days"
—All-caps brand name: SALT DEVELOPMENT
—Emoji as decoration or icons
