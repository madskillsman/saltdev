You don't pay your monthly fee until you reach the front page for the terms we agree on. A one-time onboarding fee covers the upfront build, and we go over it on the call. It depends on your market and competition. Some businesses move in weeks, competitive ones take longer. We give you an honest timeline on the call, based on your situation, not a generic promise.
