## 6. Final CTA + FAQ

### Final CTA

Heading: Find out if you qualify
Body: If your business is strong but hard to find, the front page is the fastest way to grow. Book a call and we'll tell you straight whether the guarantee fits.
Button: Book a call

### FAQ

Q: How does "don't pay until you rank" work?
A: You don't pay your monthly fee until you reach the front page for the terms we agree on. A one-time onboarding fee covers the upfront build, and we go over it on the call. We set the ranking terms with you before any work starts, so there is no moving goalpost. Until you rank, the ongoing risk sits with us.

Q: How long until I rank?
A: It depends on your market and competition. Some businesses move in weeks, competitive ones take longer. We give you an honest timeline on the call, based on your situation, not a generic promise.

Q: Do you build websites too?
A: Yes. SEO is the focus, but when your site is holding back results we build a fast, clean one that supports your rankings instead of fighting them.
