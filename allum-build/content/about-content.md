## Section: The why

Heading: Why I started Allum

Body:
I started Allum after watching a good company fall apart. The work was solid. The owner was respected. It still collapsed, because he never learned how to bring in new business. The customers couldn't find him.

That stuck with me. Most service businesses have the same gap. They're good at what they do and they rely on referrals, which works right up until it doesn't. When referrals soften, there's no system to replace them.

That's the problem I fix. I get businesses found, trusted, and contacted by people who were never referred.

Pull quote:
"My mission is to help people who are good at what they do make more money."

## Section: How I work

Heading: How I work

Body:
SEO is the core of what I do. I build the structure that gets you ranking, then back it with a guarantee: you don't pay your monthly fee until you're on the front page.

I don't churn out template sites or hand over a report and disappear. I stay involved after launch, because rankings and results are something you maintain, not something you buy once.

I build on WordPress and Bricks. Fast, clean, no bloat. I'd rather do fewer things well than ship a pile of work that doesn't perform.
