## Build notes

- Use categories as the internal-linking backbone. Each category should have its own archive URL.
- This hub is where future programmatic pages (persona, location) live or link from.
- Quality over volume. A few strong, genuinely useful posts beat a churn of thin ones.
- Each post needs a unique title tag, meta description, and one clear topic.
