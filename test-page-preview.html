Worth trying

Three small rituals, zero subscription fees

A short, unscientific list of analog habits that punch well above their weight. Pick one and run it for a week.

01

Write one letter

A real, handwritten note to someone who'd never expect it. Slower than a text, and remembered far longer.

02

Brew tea on purpose

No multitasking. Just the kettle, the steam, and three minutes that belong only to you.

03

Take the long way

A ten-minute walk with no destination clears more mental clutter than most productivity hacks.
